Your right to cancel a sale made at your home

You may cancel this transaction, without any penalty or obligation, within three business days from the date you signed. This applies to any sale of more than $25 made at your home.

Para cancelar, envíe por correo o entregue el aviso de cancelación, o llámenos, antes de la medianoche del tercer día hábil.

How the three-day right works

Under California's Home Solicitation Sales Act (Civil Code §1689.5 and following) and the Federal Trade Commission's Cooling-Off Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 429), a sale of more than $25 made at your residence may be cancelled until midnight of the third business day after the date you signed. You do not need a reason.

  • Business days do not include Sundays or federal holidays
  • Your representative must give you two copies of a completed Notice of Cancellation when you sign, and a copy of your signed contract
  • If the sale was negotiated primarily in Spanish, Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese, or Korean, the notice and contract must be given to you in that language (Civil Code §1632)
  • Cancelling costs you nothing. Any payment you made is returned within 10 days of your notice
  • If equipment was delivered, make it available at your home in substantially the same condition. You are not required to ship it anywhere at your own expense

Home alarm and monitoring agreements arranged through Direct Protection Security carry the same three-day right, along with the protections in Business & Professions Code §7599 and following.

How to cancel

Any of these works, as long as it happens before midnight of the third business day:

  1. Call us at (408) 909-4484 and say you are cancelling. Note the date and time you called
  2. Email darrell@completehomellc.com with your name and service address
  3. Mail or deliver the signed notice below to the address on it. If mailed, postmark it before the deadline

Cancelling the sale with us does not, by itself, cancel an account the provider has already opened. Call us and we will confirm the cancellation with the provider — and you should also confirm directly with them.

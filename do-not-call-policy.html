Do-Not-Call Policy & Call Recording Notice

Last updated: August 26, 2026 · Maintained under 47 C.F.R. §64.1200(d)

To stop hearing from us: call (408) 909-4484, reply STOP to any text, tell any representative on a call or at your door, or email darrell@completehomellc.com. We honor the request within one business day and keep it on file permanently. You do not need to give a reason.

This is the written internal do-not-call policy of Complete Home Today LLC, maintained as required by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act and Federal Communications Commission rules. A copy is available to any person on demand — ask any representative, or contact us using the details below, and we will provide it at no cost.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to Complete Home Today LLC and to every independent contractor, representative, and person making calls or sending text messages on its behalf.

2. How to make a do-not-call request

You may ask us to stop contacting you by any of these methods, at any time:

  • Phone: (408) 909-4484
  • Text: reply STOP to any message from us
  • Email: darrell@completehomellc.com
  • Mail: Complete Home Today LLC, 36245 Capri Dr, Winchester, CA 92596
  • In person: tell any representative at your door or on a call

Give us the phone number you want suppressed. You may also ask us to suppress an email address or a service address.

3. How we handle your request

  • Requests are recorded on our internal do-not-call list within one business day, and in all cases within the 30 days required by law
  • Entries are kept indefinitely and are not removed unless you ask us in writing to resume contact
  • A request applies to Complete Home Today LLC and to anyone calling on our behalf
  • You are not required to give a reason, and making a request never affects service you already have or a quote already in progress
  • We do not sell, rent, or share our internal do-not-call list, except as necessary to enforce it

4. National and state Do Not Call registries

We access the National Do Not Call Registry and suppress registered numbers except where we have your express written consent or an established business relationship that permits contact. Registered numbers are not called for telemarketing purposes outside those exceptions. You can register your number free at donotcall.gov or by calling 1-888-382-1222.

5. Consent you give us

When you submit a form on our website or give a representative your number, you consent to be contacted about internet, TV, phone, and home alarm and monitoring services by Complete Home Today LLC and the specific companies whose programs we are authorized to represent — Frontier, EarthLink, and Direct Protection Security. That consent is never a condition of any purchase, and you can withdraw it at any time using the methods above. Withdrawing consent does not affect contact that is strictly necessary to complete a transaction you have already asked for, such as confirming an installation appointment.

6. Calling hours and identification

We do not make telemarketing calls before 8:00 a.m. or after 9:00 p.m. in your local time. At the start of every call, the representative identifies themselves by name, identifies Complete Home Today LLC as the caller, and states the purpose of the call. We transmit accurate caller ID information. A representative will never ask you for a password, PIN, Social Security number, or a cash payment.

7. Call recording and monitoring

California is a two-party consent state (Penal Code §632). Some of our calls are recorded or monitored for quality assurance, training, and to document consent.

  • When a call is being recorded, you will be told at the beginning of the call
  • If you do not want to be recorded, say so. We will stop the recording and continue the call, or end the call — your choice. Declining to be recorded never affects the service or pricing available to you
  • Recordings are used only for the purposes above, are not published or sold, and are retained no longer than needed for quality, training, and consent-documentation purposes
  • You may request deletion of a recording of your own call by contacting us

8. Text messaging

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply and are charged by your carrier. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help, or call us. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Your mobile opt-in information is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party for that party's own marketing.

9. Training and enforcement

Every representative is trained on this policy before making calls on our behalf, and is required to record and forward do-not-call requests immediately. Failure to honor a request is grounds for termination of a representative's agreement.

10. If we get it wrong

If you asked us to stop and heard from us anyway, tell us — call (408) 909-4484 or email us with the date and number. We will investigate, correct the record, and confirm back to you.

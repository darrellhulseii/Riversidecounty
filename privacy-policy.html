Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026Effective Date: August 26, 2026

Complete Home Today LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit https://completehomellc.com (the "Site"). We adopt this policy to comply with applicable privacy laws, including the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA), California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and similar regulations.

Complete Home Today LLC operates as an authorized reseller and marketing partner for internet, wireless, and connectivity programs offered by third-party service providers. We do not provide internet service directly and do not process or submit service orders through this website.

By accessing or using the Site, you agree to this Privacy Policy. Please also review our Terms & Conditions for additional information regarding your use of the Site. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

The short version. We collect what you type into our forms so an agent can quote your address, and we share it with the providers needed to fulfill that request. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your mobile opt-in with anyone for their own marketing. You can opt out or ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you fill out forms or contact us, including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Service or mailing address, including city, state, and ZIP code
  • Details about your current and desired services — such as your current provider, current speed, whether you have TV, home phone, or mobile service, number of mobile lines, approximate monthly bill, and how long you have had the service
  • Home security preferences, such as number of exterior doors, package interest, and camera or smart-equipment preferences
  • Any documents or images you choose to upload, such as a photo of a current bill (which may contain your account number, service address, and billing amounts)
  • Referral information, such as the name of the representative who visited you
  • If you apply for a contractor role: your name, city, phone, email, and any information you provide about your interest and background

We do not collect payment information or Social Security numbers, and the Site does not require user accounts or passwords.

Information collected automatically

We may automatically collect certain information through cookies and similar technologies, such as browser type, device type, IP address, referring page, pages visited, and interaction data.

Consent records

When you submit a form, we record the date and time of your submission, the page you submitted from, and the exact consent language displayed to you, together with a third-party consent certificate as described in Section 4.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and service requests
  • Check service availability and eligibility at your address
  • Connect you with third-party telecommunications or connectivity service providers
  • Facilitate referrals for eligibility review and enrollment
  • Quote and arrange home alarm and monitoring services through our monitoring partner
  • Communicate regarding services, updates, and next steps
  • Evaluate and respond to independent contractor applications
  • Improve website performance, functionality, and user experience
  • Support marketing, advertising, and remarketing efforts
  • Document consent and comply with legal, regulatory, and recordkeeping obligations

Complete Home Today LLC acts solely as a marketing and referral partner. We do not process or submit service orders directly through this website. Service providers complete all orders through separate provider-approved systems. All final service qualification, account setup, billing, and service delivery are handled exclusively by the third-party provider.

We do not sell or share your personal information for monetary consideration or cross-context behavioral advertising as defined under applicable law.

3. Communications & Consent

By submitting your information on this Site, you provide express written consent to be contacted by:

  • Complete Home Today LLC
  • Its authorized partners

This contact may include:

  • Phone calls (including automated or prerecorded calls)
  • Text messages (SMS/MMS)
  • Emails

These communications may be used for marketing, service-related updates, and enrollment purposes, and may concern offers that are not directly related to the specific campaign through which you contacted us.

Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to a text message, clicking unsubscribe in an email, asking to be removed during a call, or emailing us at the address in Section 16.

Mobile information is never shared for third-party marketing. Your mobile opt-in data and consent are not sold, rented, or shared with any third party for that party's own independent marketing purposes. Mobile information is shared only with the service providers, monitoring partner, and program administrators needed to fulfill your request, and with the subprocessors that operate our forms and messaging.

4. Consent Documentation & Verification

To verify and document your consent, a third-party service — TrustedForm, operated by ActiveProspect — records your interaction with our forms, including the page content shown to you, your form activity, IP address, and browser details, and generates a certificate of your submission. This certificate is transmitted with your inquiry and may be retained and provided to service providers, program administrators, or regulators as evidence of the consent you gave. ActiveProspect processes this information under its own privacy terms.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies to:

  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve functionality and user experience
  • Support advertising and remarketing campaigns

The Site uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, and implements Google Consent Mode. When you first visit, a cookie banner lets you choose which categories to allow:

  • Necessary — required for the Site to function; always active
  • Analytics — measures traffic and usage; off until you allow it
  • Marketing — supports advertising and remarketing; off until you allow it

Analytics and marketing storage remain disabled until you consent, and you can change your choices at any time using the "Cookie settings" control on the Site. You can also control or disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features may not function properly if disabled.

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) and similar browser-based opt-out preference signals where required by law. We do not currently respond to "Do Not Track" signals, as no common industry standard for them has been adopted.

6. How We Share Information

We may share your personal information with:

  • Service providers whose programs we represent — including Frontier and EarthLink, and their authorized program administrators — for service qualification, eligibility review, and enrollment
  • Our monitoring partner, Direct Protection Security, when you request home alarm or monitoring services
  • Vendors that support our operations, such as our form-processing provider (Web3Forms), our consent-verification provider (ActiveProspect/TrustedForm), analytics and advertising platforms, and email or hosting services
  • Legal authorities when required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, subpoenas, or lawful requests, or to protect our rights
  • A successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes without your consent, and we do not share mobile opt-in data for that purpose at all.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Canada, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our processing of your personal information, to request portability of your data, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

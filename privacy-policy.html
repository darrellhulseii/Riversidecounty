1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you fill out forms or contact us, including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Service or mailing address, including city, state, and ZIP code
  • Details about your current and desired services — such as your current provider, current speed, whether you have TV, home phone, or mobile service, number of mobile lines, and how long you have had the service
  • The plan you are interested in and the promotion you are asking about, where you select one or tap an offer
  • Whether you asked us to contact you later if fiber becomes available at your address
  • Home security preferences, such as number of exterior doors, package interest, and camera or smart-equipment preferences
  • Referral information, such as the name of the representative who visited you
  • If you apply for a contractor role: your name, city, phone, email, and any information you provide about your interest and background
  • If you submit a review: your name, city, rating, review text, email address, the name of the representative who assisted you, and any photograph you choose to upload
  • The exact monthly total of your current service, if you provide it — a figure you type, not a document

We do not collect payment information, bank or card details, Social Security numbers, government identification, or health information, and the Site does not require user accounts or passwords. Please do not send us any of that information. If it reaches us anyway, we delete it rather than store it.

We do not accept copies of your bill. The only upload this website accepts is an optional photograph attached to a customer review. To compare your current service we ask for the exact monthly total as a number you type — that is sufficient, and it means no account number, billing address, or statement image ever reaches us.

A note about photographs

Photographs can carry embedded metadata, including the date, device, and sometimes the GPS coordinates where the image was taken. The review photo upload strips this metadata automatically on your own device, before the file is sent: the image is re-encoded in your browser so that only the visible picture is transmitted, and the original file with its metadata never reaches our systems. It is an automated safeguard rather than a guarantee, and it cannot remove information that is visible in the picture itself — so please still review any photo before uploading, and avoid images showing house numbers, street signs, bills, account numbers, or alarm equipment placement.

Information collected automatically

We may automatically collect certain information through cookies and similar technologies, such as browser type, device type, IP address, referring page, pages visited, and interaction data.

Consent records

When you submit a form, we record the date and time of your submission, the page you submitted from, and the exact consent language displayed to you, together with a third-party consent certificate as described in Section 4.

5. Reviews & Photographs You Submit

If you submit a review through this Site, you are giving us information that is intended to be published. Please read this section before submitting.

  • What becomes public. With your consent, we may publish your review text, the first name and last initial you provide, your city, the representative name you provide, and any photograph you upload — on this website, on our social media accounts, and in marketing materials.
  • What stays private. Your email address is never published. We use it only to verify that a review is genuine, to contact you with a question, or to confirm a removal request.
  • Moderation. A person reviews every submission before publication. We are not obligated to publish any submission, and we may decline or remove one. Declining to publish has no effect on your service.
  • Nothing is paid for. We do not compensate or incentivize reviews. See our Terms & Conditions, Section 14, for the full terms that apply to submitted content.
  • Withdrawing consent. You may ask us to remove your review or photograph at any time by emailing us. We will remove it from our website promptly. Content that has already been reshared, screenshotted, or cached by third parties may persist beyond our control, and we cannot guarantee its removal from those places.
  • Adults only. Reviews may be submitted only by individuals 18 or older, and photographs must not show a minor without the consent of their parent or legal guardian.
  • Nothing publishes automatically. This website has no database and no automatic publishing. A review appears on the site only after a person has read it and manually added it, which means no submission is ever visible to other visitors before it has been checked.
  • Representative names. If you name the representative who helped you, we publish their first name and last initial only, under the consent given in their contractor agreement.
  • Retention. A published review and the consent record authorizing it are kept while the review is displayed and for a reasonable period afterward. Submissions we decline to publish are deleted.
  • Your honest opinion is protected. Consistent with the federal Consumer Review Fairness Act, we will never penalize you, charge you, or change your service because of a negative review.

Published reviews are publicly visible and may be indexed by search engines. Please do not include personal information about yourself or anyone else — including your street address, account number, or phone number — in the text of a review.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies to:

  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve functionality and user experience
  • Support advertising and remarketing campaigns

Where you have allowed analytics or marketing cookies, we record a small number of events describing how the Site is used: which offer or plan buttons are tapped, when a form is started, and when one is successfully submitted. These events carry the plan or promotion name and the rating you selected on a review — they never carry your name, address, phone number, email, or the text of anything you wrote. Your form details go to us and to the providers fulfilling your request, never to an analytics or advertising platform.

The Site uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, and implements Google Consent Mode. When you first visit, a cookie banner lets you choose which categories to allow:

  • Necessary — required for the Site to function; always active
  • Analytics — measures traffic and usage; off until you allow it
  • Marketing — supports advertising and remarketing; off until you allow it

Analytics and marketing storage remain disabled until you consent, and you can change your choices at any time using the "Cookie settings" control on the Site. You can also control or disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features may not function properly if disabled.

Global Privacy Control. We detect the GPC opt-out preference signal on every page load. When one is present, marketing cookies and advertising sharing are disabled and locked for that visit, the marketing option in our cookie banner is switched off and greyed out, and an "accept all" click cannot re-enable it. A notice appears in the banner confirming this. We treat a GPC signal as a valid opt-out request under the CPRA.

We do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals, as no common industry standard for them has been adopted.

13. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS) for every page and form submission
  • A Content Security Policy and strict referrer policy that limit what third-party code can run on or receive data from this Site
  • Automatic removal of embedded metadata, including GPS coordinates, from uploaded photographs before they leave your device
  • Collection limited to what is needed: we do not collect payment card numbers, bank details, or Social Security numbers, and the Site has no user accounts or passwords to be compromised
  • Access to submitted inquiries limited to personnel who need it to respond
  • Automated abuse checks on our forms — a hidden field and a minimum time-on-form — that stop scripted spam without profiling you, tracking you, or collecting anything extra about you
  • No database of visitor information on this website: form submissions are transmitted to us and are not stored on the site, and published reviews are added manually rather than by an automated system

No system can guarantee complete security. If a breach affecting your personal information occurs, we will notify affected individuals and any required regulators in accordance with applicable law, including California Civil Code §1798.82.

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in this Site, please report it to darrell@completehomellc.com rather than disclosing it publicly. We will investigate and will not pursue action against good-faith researchers who avoid privacy violations and service disruption.
